# I.M. Barruga — Civil Engineering Portfolio

**Live site:** [im-barruga.vercel.app](https://im-barruga.vercel.app)

A portfolio site designed around a single idea: present a civil engineer's work the way a civil engineer would — as a set of drawings. Rather than a conventional resume-style layout, the site is built to feel like an architectural drawing sheet come to life, carrying that language through its intro, navigation, and project presentation.

---

## Concept

The site opens on a blueprint that draws itself from walls, columns, doors, windows, and fixtures appearing stroke by stroke on a dark drafting-table background, complete with a title block, dimension notes, and revision tag, exactly as they'd appear on a real construction document. That floor plan then pans smoothly into the hero section, carrying the visual language of the drawing board through the rest of the site.

The result is a portfolio that doesn't just describe technical drafting work — it looks and feels like the medium itself.

---

## Showcase

**An animated architectural intro** that sets the tone before a single word of copy is read, transitioning from a blank grid to a fully annotated floor plan to the hero section.

**An interactive project gallery**, where each entry opens as a full, page-navigable drawing set rather than a static image, letting visitors review actual architectural and structural documentation: floor plans, elevations, sections, and site development plans across six featured projects spanning residential, structural, fire protection, and interdisciplinary work.

**A documented design process**, walking through the five stages a project moves through — from site investigation and conceptual layout, to CAD drafting, structural coordination, and final as-built documentation.

**A structured academic and experience timeline**, presenting education, freelance work, on-the-job training, and professional affiliations in a clean, chronological track.

**A skills breakdown**, pairing software proficiency (rendered as animated skill bars) with core technical competencies.

**A working contact section**, with a direct inquiry form and resume download, built to convert portfolio visits into freelance opportunities.

Every section is built with scroll-triggered reveals, so the page unfolds progressively as a visitor moves through it — mirroring the pacing of walking through a real drawing set.

---

## Design Language

- **Dark, drafting-table palette** with teal linework and gold accents, evoking CAD software and blueprint conventions.
- **Monospace and technical typefaces** (Space Grotesk, Space Mono) alongside a serif display face (Cormorant Garamond) for contrast between technical precision and personal voice.
- **Consistent annotation styling** — dimension lines, title blocks, and drawing labels — used throughout, not just in the intro.
