How I Work

Design Process

01
Site Investigation & Programming
Gather lot data, technical descriptions, and client requirements. Establish setbacks, easements, and the spatial program before a single line is drawn.
02
Conceptual Layout & Sketching
Rough floor plans and massing studies to test circulation, unit layouts, and structural grid options against the site constraints.
03
CAD Drafting & Detailing
Full AutoCAD production set: floor plans, elevations, sections, and details, drawn to scale with consistent line weights and annotation standards.
04
Structural & Systems Coordination
Foundation, framing, and utility routing (fire & sprinkler, plumbing) checked against architectural drawings to catch clashes before they reach the field.
05
Review, Revision & As-Built Documentation
Markups incorporated, sheets finalized, and (for completed projects) field verification against the as-built condition.
